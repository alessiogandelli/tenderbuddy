# Ender brand kit

The end of tenders.

- `Ender-brand-guide.pdf`: the rules on one page (logo, icon, colour, type).
- `logo/`: the wordmark with the mint end mark. SVG for screens and print, PNG for slides and documents.
  - `light`: navy on light backgrounds (primary). `dark`: white on navy.
  - `mono` / `mono-white`: one colour, when mint is not available.
- `icon/`: the constructed E on a navy tile. App icon, avatar, favicon (SVG, PNG 16 to 1024).
- `colour/`: `tokens.css` (CSS variables, light and dark theme) and `tokens.json`.
- `fonts/`: IBM Plex Sans and Plex Mono (OFL licence). Also on Google Fonts.

Colours: Ink #002439 · Mint #70BD95 · Teal #1B7350 · Paper #F4F7F6 · Slate #4F6875.
Never stretch or recolour the logo, never drop the mint square. Minimum width 72 px / 20 mm;
below that, use the icon.
